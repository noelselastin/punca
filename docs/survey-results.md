# APU student survey results

22 APU STEM students answered the team's Google Form, the *APU Student Learning Experience Survey*, by 12 November 2026. 82% have struggled with an advanced topic because they did not fully understand an earlier concept, and 91% would find a platform like Punca useful.

It is a small, self-selected sample. It shows the problem exists on our campus, not how common it is across APU. Counts below are worked back from the percentages in the Google Forms summary (share × 22). Question 1 is not included here.
